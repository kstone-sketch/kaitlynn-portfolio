The idea

For years I've shared cat-friendly, rental-friendly home ideas on TikTok and Pinterest, and followers kept asking for help with their own spaces. Kikko Design Co. turns that into a business: DIY kits, guided builds or done-for-you design, one space at a time.

What I built

All of it: the name and brand book, the website and booking intake, a cat personality quiz, a mini game, the social launch and the email lifecycle. Designed and written by me, coded with Claude Code.

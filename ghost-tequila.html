2020 Needs a Kick in the Glass

When the bars closed, answer a stressed-out country with a joke instead of silence.

The line ran on roughly 300 billboards in Texas, Massachusetts and Rhode Island, ad space that went cheap when everyone else went quiet, backed by digital, social, print and PR as Ghost pivoted from bars to retail.

my role
Campaign creative, social and paid rollout
channels
OOH · digital · paid social · print · PR
result
Sales tripled; the worst-case year became the best

Raise Your Spirit: Halloween 2020

Make Ghost the season's go-to pour with a full Halloween takeover.

Thirty "Raise Your Spirit" boxes went to top costume and makeup creators, Orlando ran spooky digital billboards, and Pinterest, Google and paid social pushed a menu of scary-good cocktails.

my role
Concept, photoshoot direction, creator PR boxes
channels
Creator boxes · OOH · social · Pinterest · Google · PR
result

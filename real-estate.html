Three Northern Virginia developments, three very different buyers. I led social and creative for each one, building a voice that could sell a lifestyle before the doors opened.

Objective

Give three developments in the same market three distinct identities: a boutique condo in historic Old Town Alexandria, a high-rise in Tysons Corner and a 424-acre live-work-play community in Loudoun County. Each needed to win buyers, residents or businesses ahead of launch.

My role

I led social strategy and execution across all three, from brand voice and messaging to paid social, print, email and lifestyle video, working with design teams to keep every touchpoint consistent while each brand stayed its own.
